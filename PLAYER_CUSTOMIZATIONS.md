# Player customizations

This fork includes two small player-focused changes:

1. The Now Playing scrubber represents progress through the entire book, weighted by section text length, and seeking on the scrubber jumps across the whole book rather than only within the current section.
2. The lyrics/text view uses tighter line spacing and smaller top/bottom fades so long chunks stay visible and scrollable on mobile without being obscured by the player controls.

The TTS engines, EPUB parsing, and model behavior are otherwise unchanged.
